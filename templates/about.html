About Us & Constitution Guide

A shared reference for our purpose, membership commitments, lending, welfare, and leadership.

Who we are

Our purpose and aims

01

Our mission

The Group's mission is to foster mutual support, collective well-being, and financial security among its members.

02

Our objectives

We promote financial discipline and collective saving while encouraging members to support one another.

03

Our aims

We aim to provide financial assistance and solidarity to members during both certainties and uncertainties.

Member reference

Membership & group rules

Use these guide figures alongside the current constitution and decisions formally adopted by the group.

Joining fee

UGX 10,000

One-time membership fee.

Monthly subscription

UGX 20,000

Regular monthly member contribution.

Full membership

5 months · UGX 100,000

Five monthly contributions of UGX 20,000 complete the UGX 100,000 welfare contribution required for full membership.

Loan limit

Up to 3× savings

Subject to eligibility and approval, with a current system application cap of UGX 300,000. Interest is capped at 10% per month.

Welfare support

Certainties

Wedding and traditional introduction support is for the member only.

Uncertainties

Illness: member, or a registered spouse or child marked Alive.

Death: member, or a registered spouse, child, parent, or parent-in-law marked Alive.

Relatives must be registered before a claim is submitted. Executives still review every claim.

Meeting fines

Fine amounts and attendance rules are to be confirmed against the current constitution and committee decisions.

Details pending confirmation

If a figure or rule in this guide differs from the current constitution or a formally approved resolution, follow the governing document and confirmed group decision.
