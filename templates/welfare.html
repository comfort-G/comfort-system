Welfare support at a glance

Check who qualifies and how your request is handled before submitting.

To apply, you must be past probation and have a welfare balance of at least UGX 40,000.

01

Wedding & introduction

Wedding and traditional introduction support is for the member only.

02

Illness support

Eligible for the member or a registered spouse or child marked Alive.

03

Death support

Eligible for the member or a registered spouse, child, parent, or parent-in-law marked Alive.

04

Review & decision

Welfare staff or the Treasurer begin the review. The Chairperson approves or rejects the claim, then the Treasurer records any disbursement.

05

How support is funded

Each disbursement assesses up to UGX 20,000 per eligible member, using welfare balances first and available savings next. Members who are on probation when a claim is submitted keep their balances and are exempt from that claim: they are not charged or given arrears for it, even after probation ends.
